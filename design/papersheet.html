05 — Pomegranates

Iran does not grow
one pomegranate.

Five profiles · EU market

The five selected varieties are five distinct product profiles — different combinations of fruit size, skin and aril colour, sweetness, acidity and seed texture. The buyer's question is which of the five, so the figures below are set out to be read against each other.

Saveh Deep red, large and balanced. The established Iranian export pomegranate and the reference point for the other four. 300–450 G · 18–19 °BX · ARIL 55–62 %
Neyriz The size variety. Red to crimson, firm and thick-skinned, with the highest soluble solids in the range. 440–550 G · 18–18.5 °BX · ARIL 46–52 %
Ferdows Large red fruit with a wide aril-yield range and a firm structure built for distribution. 350–490 G · ≈ 18 °BX · ARIL 52–65 %
Yazd The outlier. Very large, sweeter, lighter in skin, and the highest aril and juice yields of the five. 370–700 G · 16–17 °BX · ARIL 58–74 %
Badrud The versatile commercial profile. Medium-large, firm, balanced, and the easiest of the five to place. 280–350 G · 16–17 °BX · ARIL 58–63 %
Sugar splits the five

Saveh, Neyriz and Ferdows sit at or above 18 °Brix; Yazd and Badrud at 16–17. That gap is the clearest dividing line in the range.

Yield is where Yazd wins

Highest on both aril and juice yield. For fresh-cut and juice programmes that is worth more than the two Brix points it gives away.

Harvest

Fars and central Iran, September to November. Figures describe commercial profiles and are confirmed per lot and season.

Quality & grading
01

Three EU classes

Extra, Class I and Class II under the EU/UNECE marketing standard, separated by minimum fruit weight — 90 g, 70 g and 65 g.

02

Calibres 30 – 60

Calibre is the fruit count per package, so a lower number is a heavier fruit. Class follows weight, not preference: calibres 54 and 60 sit below the 70 g Class I floor and are declared Class II.

03

6.2 – 9.0 °Brix at packing

Or 15–17 % dry matter. Either target on its own demonstrates commercial maturity; they are not cumulative.

04

Zero tolerance

Rot, visible mould, serious bruising and insect damage are not permitted at any class.
