Five profiles, not one product

Iran does not grow one pomegranate.

Iran is home to a diverse range of commercial pomegranates, offering different combinations of fruit size, skin colour, aril colour, sweetness, acidity and seed texture.

Our selected varieties represent five distinct product profiles — from deep-red, balanced sweet-tart fruit to exceptionally large and sweet pomegranates.

Yazd spans the widest weight range

370–700 g is nearly double from the bottom of the range to the top, which makes calibre selection more consequential for Yazd than for any other variety here.

Sugar splits the five in two

Saveh, Neyriz and Ferdows sit at or above 18 °Brix. Yazd and Badrud sit at 16–17. That gap is the clearest dividing line in the range.

Fruit weight is set on the tree. Grading sorts what the orchard produced; it does not create the top of a variety's range.

Yield is where Yazd earns its place

Highest on both aril and juice yield. For fresh-cut and juice programmes that is worth more than the two Brix points it gives away.

Neyriz trades yield for size

The lowest aril yield of the five, and the largest, firmest, thickest-skinned fruit. It is bought for presentation, not for processing.

What the percentages are

Aril yield is the share of the whole fruit that is edible aril; juice yield is the share that presses out. Both are measured on the fruit, not estimated from its size — which is why a large variety does not automatically lead on either.

Calibre & packing

Sized to the order,
not to a fixed table

We do not publish a fixed calibre. For the European market, size can be specified by fruit count, by diameter or by individual fruit weight — and the UNECE standard for pomegranate separates size codes by weight.

A UNECE size code for pomegranate, given as an example of weight-based sizing.

The band most of the large-fruited varieties in this range fall into.
