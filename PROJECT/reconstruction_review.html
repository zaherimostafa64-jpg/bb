Seven measurements

Rather than a long survey list, these are the fewest measurements that move the most geometry. Taking all seven is projected to lift overall confidence from 55 % to about 88 %.

  1. 01Hall ceiling height Floor to ceiling, measured twice: under the flat soffit, and inside the tray cove. all vertical dims
  2. 02Hall length Dining‑end wall to living‑end wall. This is the one that resolves conflict C‑003. largest error
  3. 03Hall depth Window facade across to the kitchen and entrance partition. hall area
  4. 04Main entrance door width Structural opening, not the leaf. Tightens the whole entrance zone. entrance zone
  5. 05Kitchen, both dimensions Confirms or corrects the derived work‑run build‑up. kitchen box
  6. 06Pass‑through opening Clear width and sill height above the floor. calibration check
  7. 07Fireplace blade Overall length, thickness, and the width of the panelled column. alcove opening
05

Open questions

C‑001 · Two fireplaces

A brick firebox faces the hall in four images and the alcove in four others, from opposite sides of the blade. Modelled as one blade with a recess in each face, sharing a flue. Confirm on site.

C‑002 · Kitchen windows

Two windows with a pier between them are unambiguous. A third, larger window appears in one frame only and may be the same pair read across a panorama seam.

C‑003 · Hall length

The two end‑to‑end views disagree by about two metres. The mid‑value is carried. Everything downstream of this number inherits the error.

C‑004 · Orientation

No compass information exists in the set. The drawings carry a project north only, marked ASSUMED. Kitchen windows face mountains, hall windows face roofscape — consistent with a corner unit, but the bearing is unrecoverable.

Panoramic distortion

Every source frame is an ultra‑wide stitched panorama. No line was traced directly; geometry was recovered only from features repeated across several viewpoints, using each frame’s central third.

Out of scope

The corridor past its threshold and the entire private zone were not photographed and are not modelled. The drawings mark the cut with a scope boundary.
